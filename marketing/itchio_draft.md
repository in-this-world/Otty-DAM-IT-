# 🦦 Otty, DAM IT!

The river is rising. The dam is not done. And *somebody* just ate the fish instead of carrying sticks.

**Otty, DAM IT!** is a fast, silly co-op party game about otters building a dam before the flood countdown hits zero. Grab sticks, haul rocks, slap them onto the dam — and maybe poke a friend or two along the way. Every round is 3–5 minutes of teamwork, betrayal, and belly-floating.

Finish the dam before the water arrives and everyone wins. Fail, and the flood washes you all away — but at least the scoreboard will tell you exactly whose fault it was.

### ✨ Features

- 🌊 **One goal, one timer** — get the dam to 100% before the flood. That's it. You'll get it in 3 seconds.
- ⏱️ **3–5 minute rounds** — perfect "one more round" length.
- 🌿 **Sticks are tools AND weapons** — build with them, or poke a friend and watch them drop everything.
- 🐟 **Fish: eat it for a speed boost, or throw it at someone's face.** Both are valid strategies. One is funnier.
- 🚧 **Wear a traffic cone.** It's eagle-proof headwear and a fashion statement.
- 🦅🐻 **Random events** — eagles dive, bears wander in, everybody panics.
- 🤖 **Play solo with AI otters** — no friends online? The bots will carry (and occasionally sabotage) you.
- 🌐 **Zero install** — runs in your browser, on desktop and mobile.

### 🚧 In development

This is a **pre-alpha work in progress**. Currently playable: the single-player core loop (build the dam, beat the flood, with AI otters). Coming later in development:

- **2–10 player online multiplayer** — join a room with one shared link, no account needed
- More items, more chaos events, hand-holding otter rafts
- Final art, sound and music

Follow the project to get notified when new builds drop. Feedback is very welcome — tell us what made you laugh and what made you rage.

# 🦦 水獺蓋水壩 Otty, DAM IT!

洪水倒數中,水壩蓋一半,結果隊友在旁邊吃魚。

**《水獺蓋水壩》**是一款節奏超快的合作派對遊戲:一群水獺要趕在洪水來之前,把樹枝、石頭統統搬去蓋水壩。一局 3–5 分鐘,大家一起努力——或者拿樹枝戳隊友,看他手上的建材噴滿地。

倒數歸零前水壩蓋到 100%,全體獲勝;蓋不完,大水沖走一切,結算畫面會誠實告訴大家:到底是誰在混。

### ✨ 特色

- 🌊 **目標只有一個**——洪水來之前把水壩蓋好。三秒就懂,上手零門檻。
- ⏱️ **一局 3–5 分鐘**,「再來一局」剛剛好。
- 🌿 **樹枝既是建材也是武器**——可以蓋壩,也可以戳人,被戳到東西掉滿地。
- 🐟 **魚可以吃(加速),也可以砸人(暈眩)。**都合理,但砸人比較好笑。
- 🚧 **三角錐戴頭上**,防老鷹又時尚。
- 🦅🐻 **隨機突發事件**:老鷹俯衝、熊出沒,全場雞飛狗跳。
- 🤖 **一個人也能玩**——AI 水獺隊友幫你搬(偶爾也扯你後腿)。
- 🌐 **免下載免安裝**,瀏覽器打開就玩,電腦手機都行。

### 🚧 開發中

目前為 **pre-alpha 開發初期版本**:單機核心玩法(蓋壩、倒數、AI 水獺)可玩。之後的開發計畫:

- **2–10 人線上同樂**——一條連結進房,免帳號免註冊
- 更多道具、更多突發事件、手牽手水獺筏
- 正式美術、音效與音樂

追蹤本頁面就能收到更新通知。歡迎留言告訴我們:哪裡好笑、哪裡想摔鍵盤。

## How to play(頁面內小節,兩語)

### 🎮 How to Play / 怎麼玩

**EN**
1. **Move** — WASD / arrow keys (virtual joystick on mobile)
2. **Pick up / drop** — walk near an item, press the interact key
3. **Build** — carry materials to the dam site and press build
4. **Win** — dam reaches 100% before the flood countdown ends
5. Optional: poke, throw fish, wear cone. Chaos is a feature.

**ZH-TW**
1. **移動**——WASD / 方向鍵(手機用虛擬搖桿)
2. **撿取/放下**——靠近物品按互動鍵
3. **建造**——把建材搬到壩址按建造鍵
4. **獲勝**——倒數結束前水壩完成度達 100%
5. 進階玩法:戳人、丟魚、戴三角錐。混亂是特色,不是 bug。

> ⚠️ 操作鍵位以實際 build 為準,發布前與 Developer 確認(MASTER_PLAN 提及 WASD+JK / 手機搖桿+兩鍵)。
